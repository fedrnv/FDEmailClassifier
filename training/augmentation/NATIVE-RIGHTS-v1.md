# fdec-native-templates-v1: source authorship and rights assessment

The project owner requested a distinct original native template pack and
Apache-2.0 licensing. New phrase/grammar resources and the variation renderer were
written in this Codex session. They are not a renamed or copied fixture catalog,
imported email dataset, real correspondence or human-authored contribution.
Authorship is recorded as `codex-assisted-original-native-grammar`.

The original project source is provided under LICENSE/NOTICE (Apache-2.0), as
authorized by the owner's instruction. This statement covers the project source
grant to the extent applicable; it does not establish independent human
copyrightability, worldwide uniqueness, or a waiver of provider restrictions.
Only fabricated FDEC identity labels, account/reference identifiers and reserved
mail.invalid contacts are inserted by the renderer. No personal source data,
mailbox, address book or external text-generation service is read at runtime.

The [OpenAI Services Agreement](https://openai.com/policies/services-agreement/)
assigns output rights subject to its restrictions. Its classifier exception has
conditions concerning model distribution and third-party commercial availability.
The [current Usage Policies](https://openai.com/policies/usage-policies/) retain
agreement exceptions and restrict preparation of training material for substitute
models. This audit does not determine that the project is necessarily prohibited;
it records that applicable account terms or project-specific permission covering
the intended use have not been established in the repository.

Consequently this exact pack is admitted for software_fixture only. Commercial
training and evaluation are unresolved and rejected. No real provider permission
has been supplied by a new package name, original wording, synthetic identities,
Apache-2.0 header, implementation test or owner instruction to create the pack.
The owner was asked for the applicable agreement or permission document without
credentials. Such evidence must actually resolve the intended use before a new
bound audit can authorize commercial_training. Source originality and a source
license are not substituted for that missing evidence.

The previous codex-fixture-v1 catalog, audit and rights document retain their
exact hashes and commercial-training denial. The native pack has a separate
catalog, audit and evidence binding; the existing admission checks are unchanged.
There is no purpose override or automatic approval. No task closure or model/data
publication is asserted. Every generated native sample and receipt carries its
actual admitted purpose, honest origin and training_allowed status.

FDEC-011 remains open until the positive commercial-purpose acceptance criterion
is supported by real documentary grounds. Negative admission tests can prove
safe refusal; they cannot turn that missing positive evidence into a PASS.
