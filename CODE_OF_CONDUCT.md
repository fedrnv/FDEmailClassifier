# Code of conduct

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and
other project spaces, including representation of the project in public.

Be respectful, constructive and considerate. Explain technical disagreements with
evidence, accept corrections and respect people's boundaries. Welcome contributors
regardless of background, identity, experience or ability.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks,
publication of private information and retaliation against reporters are prohibited.
Do not post private email content or credentials in project discussions.

Report an incident privately to repository owner **fedrnv** using the private
contact route on https://github.com/fedrnv. Include only information necessary to
investigate; do not expose a reporter in a public issue. If that route is
unavailable, wait for a private channel instead of publishing sensitive details.
Security vulnerabilities use SECURITY.md.

Maintainers investigate confidentially to the extent possible, hear relevant
participants and apply proportionate action: a reminder, removal of content,
temporary participation restriction or permanent exclusion. Serious threats or
repeated violations may lead directly to exclusion. A participant may request
reconsideration privately from the repository owner with new evidence. Maintainers
must disclose conflicts of interest and avoid handling a report about themselves.
No response-time or staffed moderation service is promised.
